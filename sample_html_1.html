Offline-First Architecture

Care doesn’t stop when the Wi-Fi does.

Care homes are notorious for dead zones. CarePool is built as a Progressive Web App (PWA) with native-like offline capabilities. Caregivers can log medication, update notes, and check allergies without an internet connection. The moment the device reconnects, everything syncs securely and instantly. Zero data loss. Zero stress.
